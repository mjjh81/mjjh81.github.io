What Supports Birds?

Nest boxes is a common strategy to support the reproductive cycle of birds. However, they require maintenance in order to reduce the likelihood of hosting pest birds, like European Starlings, and parasites.

Perches adjacent to a field can attract birds of prey, giving them an opportunity to identify and catch rodent prey in the fields.

Reducing the use of pesticides like neonicotinoids and rodenticides will reduce direct bird mortality and also increase the presence, and therefore benefits, of birds in agricultural lands.
