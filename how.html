Birds Managing Pests

Globally birds are estimated to consume 400-500 million metric tons per year [Nyffeler 2018]. That's about the equivalent weight of the roughly 8.1 billion humans on Earth [Greenspoon 2022]. A single family of barn owls can eat 1,000-3,000 rodents a year.

There's a growing body of literature studying how birds can directly benefits farms. For example, a University of California study showed that alfalfa growers with trees near the edges of their fields attracted more birds which resulted in a 30% reduction of insect pest damage.

Birds can even help manage each other- a study in Michigan sweet cherry orchards showed how Kestrels decreasaed the presence of fruit-eating bird abundance [Shave and Lindell 2017].
