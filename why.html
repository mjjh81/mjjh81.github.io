Farms -> Birds -> Farms

Private lands are over 50% of the land in the US [Winters-Michaud 2024] representing an important habitat for bird conservation. Although the approaches to bird conservation, namely land-sparing vs land-sharing, are still being debated, both hold potential to benefit both farms and birds.

Ecosystem services are the benefits people receive from ecosystems [Millenium Ecosystem Assessment 2005]. Birds most directly benefits farms through pest management of insects and rodents.

However, birds can also offer more indirect services like dispersing seeds throughout a forest to maintain forest health and diversity, and attracting ecotourism through birdwatching and tourism.
